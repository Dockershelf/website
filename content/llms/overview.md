Overview of Dockershelf.

Problem: teams need trustworthy, lightweight base images for common languages and tools without maintaining every Dockerfile themselves.

Audience: developers and operators who pull from Docker Hub or extend images with FROM in their own Dockerfiles.

Value: weekly rebuilt and tested shelves for Debian, Python, Node, Go, and LaTeX, with stable (production) and unstable (development) tracks where applicable, on amd64 and arm64.
